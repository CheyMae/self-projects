White Elephant as tradition

  1. Bring one wrapped gift worth about ₱1,000.
  2. Everyone draws a number to set the turn order.
  3. On your turn, open a new gift or steal one that's already been opened.
  4. If your gift gets stolen, you pick again: open a new one or steal another.

Steal limits and other house rules will be announced on the day.
